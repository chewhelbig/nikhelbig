Gestalt in one breath

Born from the work of Fritz & Laura Perls and Paul Goodman in the 1940s–50s.

Its roots: Gestalt psychology, phenomenology, Kurt Lewin's field theory, and Buber's I–Thou dialogue.

The Gestalt move

Less about what is wrong — more about what is happening, right now, between us.
